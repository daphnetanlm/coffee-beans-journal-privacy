Frequently Asked Questions

How do I back up my beans?

Go to Settings and tap "Back up to iCloud Drive" or "Back up to Files app" to save a ZIP backup of all your beans and photos.

How do I restore from a backup?

Go to Settings and tap "Restore from backup". Select your ZIP backup file to restore all your beans and photos.

How do I unlock unlimited beans?

Tap the banner at the top of My Beans or try to add a 6th bean — you will see the unlock screen. The one-time purchase of $3.99 unlocks unlimited entries forever.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

On the unlock screen tap "Restore previous purchase" to restore your purchase on a new device or after reinstalling the app.

My data is gone after reinstalling — what happened?

App data is stored locally on your device. If you reinstalled the app without backing up first, the data cannot be recovered. We recommend backing up regularly using the Settings screen.
